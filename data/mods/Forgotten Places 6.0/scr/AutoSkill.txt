AutoSkill script is designed to give skills to proto critters. It adjusts the skill level to match a critter's corresponding stat, e.g. it will give bow 19 to a critter with dx 19. It should be attached to either 13 (enter combat) or 19 (heartbeat) attachment points. Script removes itself automatically upon giving the required skills.

3200 script should be used with counters.
Counter 3: Stat
Counter 2: Divisor
Counter 1: Effect

Example: 1 4 199. In this case a critter with dx 12 will get the effect 199 (melee +4, dodge +4) 3 times. NOTE: it's impossible to use counters with values bigger than 255, that's why premade scripts are used.
