1. Introduction
---------------

Welcome, and thank you for choosing to be part of the Arcanum Beta and Press Demo.  We at Troika have worked hard these past two years to develop a truly in-depth CRPG that would allow you, the end-user, to create and develop a character uniquely yours, to role-play as you saw fit, and to enjoy unique experiences in the world of Arcanum based upon your choices.  It was our goal to allow you as much freedom as possible, while providing a deep story and rich tapestry of events to enhance your gaming experience.

Arcanum has been designed to work with multiple styles of characters.  A few of these include, but are not limited to, thieving, diplomatic, technological, magickal and fighting character styles.  All styles of play are available regardless of the race and gender choices you have made.  For instance, an elf, being more magickal, would have a more difficult time mastering technology than a dwarf, but the option is available if you wish to pursue that type of character.

Below are just a few examples of the types of characters you may want to try out while playing the Arcanum Beta and Press Demo:

-  magickal or technological thief
-  pure thief with no magick or technological abilities
-  assassin
-  debutante or gentleman assassin/thief
-  debutante or gentleman with no violent tendencies
-  socially savvy, charismatic gambler
-  super good or super evil (mage or technologist)
-  warrior with no magick or technological skills
-  balanced character using equal amounts of magick and tech
-  gunfighter
-  inventor
-  a very dumb character
-  a very ugly character
-  a very persuasive character
-  a very charismatic or uncharismatic character

You will find that the world reacts differently to these various character types and that alternate paths as well as quests are available to them throughout the game.

Please keep in mind that this is a beta, and as such, it is still a work in progress.  We are in the process of balancing magick and technology, as well as turn based versus real time combat.  Three technological schematics, the Clockwork Decoy, the Animal Lure and the Flow Specktrometer are not functioning for the beta.  You will not be able to locate any inter-disciplinary schematics and guns are currently over powered.  Additionally, tech armors created via schematics are currently medium size only and will not fit small or large characters.  On the magickal side, Stun and Nightmare are not functioning, while Body of Air and Call Fog have incorrect art and effects.  Most of the weapons that take advantage of the Throwing skill have not yet been implemented.  You may also notice some temporary art and interfaces as you play through the game.

While playing the game, if you come across a particularly difficult combat situation, you may wish to switch to turn based mode, as it is currently more advantageous than real time mode.  Also half-orcs and half-ogres with a beauty of 2 or less are attacked on sight by the normal citizens or Arcanum.  You may wish to avoid those two extremes.

More than anything, what we are looking for from the Beta Testers is feedback on our system and the overall feel of Arcanum's gameplay.  We are looking forward to hearing from you as we work together to make Arcanum a truly great CRPG.

2. How to START the DEMO 
------------------------

Install the game. 

The AUTOPLAY feature of your CD-ROM will bring up the AUTOPLAY Menu. After you have installed the game you can select the "Play" button on this menu. 

Selecting Start\Programs\Arcanum\Arcanum Demo from your Start Menu will also start the game.

-----------------------

Please take a few moments to review the BetaManual.htm available on the CD, or via your Start Menu, for instructions on playing Arcanum.

5. Hardware compatibility issues
--------------------------------

Arcanum will try to detect your video card and use the appropriate settings for it.  If a 3D accelerator is present as the primary display adapter, Arcanum will attempt to use it.  If you have any problems, please be sure you are using the latest drivers from your chip manufacturer's website.

If the game begins, once character creation is complete, and your top and bottom menu bars are visible, but the main game window is black or distorted, this would constitute a 3D video card problem.

If you are experiencing video card related problems, you can disable 3D acceleration by exiting the game and launching Arcanum's Software Rendering Mode.  The Software Rendering Mode is available via your Start Menu.

Using the Detonator3 drivers from Nvidia may cause a problem.  Arcanum will work properly when using the Detonator2 drivers.
